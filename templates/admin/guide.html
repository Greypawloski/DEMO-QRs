System Overview

This system manages two main workflows for the SACC Tennis Shop:

  • Demo checkouts — track which demo racquets and paddles are out with customers and when they're returned.
  • Restring / string jobs — intake, track progress, and record completion of restringing work.

The top navigation bar gives you access to every section:

  • Dashboard — active demo checkouts at a glance.
  • Equipment — manage the demo inventory and QR codes.
  • History — full log of all past demo checkouts.
  • Reports — usage summaries.
  • Activity — log of all staff actions.
  • Restringing — manage all string jobs.
  • Member Search — look up and manage member contact info.
  • Kiosk View — the demo checkout screen used at the front desk.

Restring / String Jobs

Submitting a new string job

  1. Click Restringing in the top nav, then click New Restring Request.
  2. Search for the member using the Search Member field at the top. Type a name or member number — a dropdown of matches will appear. Click the correct member to auto-fill their name, member number, and phone number.
  3. If the customer is not a member, check the Non-member checkbox next to the Member Number field. The member number field will gray out.
  4. Fill in Date In, Date Promised, Racquet, String, and Tension.
  5. If the customer supplied their own string, check Customer's own string.
  6. Fill in Receipt #, Charged, and Additional Charges as applicable.
  7. Enter Strung By (the staff member doing the work) and any Notes.
  8. If the customer should not receive SMS notifications, check Do not send text alerts below the phone field.
  9. Click Save Request.
Restring history autofill: After selecting a member who has previous string jobs on file, a Load from restring history dropdown will appear above the Racquet field. Select a past job to instantly fill in the racquet, string, and tension from that visit.

Editing a string job

  1. On the Restringing page, find the job and click Edit.
  2. Make any changes and click Save Changes.

Quick-editing Charged, Receipt, and Additional Charges

On the active restrings list, you can edit the Charged, Receipt #, and Additional Charges fields without opening the full edit form:

  1. Click directly on the value (or the dash — if empty) next to Charged, Receipt, or Additional Charges on a job card.
  2. A small edit popup will appear. Type the new value and click Save.

Marking a job ready for pickup

There are two ways:

  • From the restring list: Click the Mark Ready button on the job card. Confirm the popup.
  • From the edit form: Click the green Mark Ready button next to Save Changes. Confirm the popup. The button will be grayed out if the job is already marked ready.
Marking a job ready automatically sends the customer an SMS notification that their racquet is ready for pickup. A reminder SMS is sent automatically if the racquet has not been picked up after 72 hours. Both messages are suppressed if Do not send text alerts was checked.

Marking a job picked up

  1. On the restring list, find the job in the ready section and click Mark Picked Up.
  2. The job moves to the Picked Up history at the bottom of the page.

Searching string jobs

At the top of the Restringing page there are two search bars:

  • Active jobs search — filters the active/ready list by customer name or member number.
  • Picked Up search — filters the completed history.

Deleting a string job

  1. Open the edit form for the job.
  2. Click Delete and enter the staff PIN when prompted.
Deletion requires a PIN to prevent accidental removal of records.

Demo Checkout

Accessing the kiosk

Click Kiosk View in the top nav. This is the screen used at the front desk to check out demo equipment.

Checking out a single demo

  1. On the kiosk, find the equipment card and click it.
  2. Use the Search Member field to find the customer — select them from the dropdown to auto-fill their name, member number, and phone number.
  3. If the customer is a non-member, check the Non-member checkbox.
  4. If the member number has a letter suffix (e.g. for a spouse or family member), select the letter from the dropdown next to the member number field.
  5. Enter a phone number if not already filled, add any checkout notes, and for paddles attach a condition photo if needed.
  6. Click Check Out.

Selecting multiple demos for one customer

  1. On the kiosk home screen, click Select Multiple for Checkout.
  2. Tap each equipment card you want to check out — selected cards will show a checkmark.
  3. Click Checkout Selected in the bar at the bottom.
  4. Fill in the customer details once and click Check Out. All selected items are checked out to the same customer in one step.
  5. A confirmation screen will show which items were checked out and which were skipped (if any were already unavailable).
If an item is already checked out, it will show a CHECKED OUT badge on the kiosk. You can still join the waitlist for it.

Marking a demo returned

  1. Go to Dashboard in the top nav.
  2. Find the active checkout card and click Mark Returned.
  3. Add any return notes if needed and confirm.

Undoing a return (Unmark Returned)

  1. Go to History in the top nav.
  2. Find the checkout record and click Unmark Returned.
  3. The item will go back to active checkout status. This is blocked if the equipment has already been checked out to someone else.

Uploading a condition photo (paddles)

On the checkout form for a paddle, an Upload Condition Photo button appears. Take or select a photo to document the paddle's condition at checkout. The photo is visible on the dashboard card for that checkout.

Waitlist

If a customer wants an item that is already checked out, click the item on the kiosk and select Join Waitlist. When the item is returned, the next person on the waitlist is shown on the dashboard card so staff can contact them.

Admin Dashboard

The Dashboard shows all currently active demo checkouts as cards. Each card displays:

  • Equipment name and a due-back timer (items are expected back within 72 hours — the timer turns red when overdue).
  • Customer name, member number, and phone.
  • Any checkout notes.
  • For paddles, the condition photo thumbnail.
  • Waitlist count if anyone is waiting.
  • Edit Info — update customer name, member number, phone, or notes on an active checkout.
  • Mark Returned — close out the checkout when the item is brought back.

Member Search

Searching for a member

Click Member Search in the top nav. Type in the search box — you can search by:

  • Last name prefix — e.g. Smi returns all members whose last name starts with "Smi".
  • First name — e.g. Trey returns all members with that first name.
  • First name + last name prefix — e.g. Trey F returns members named Trey whose last name starts with F.
  • Member number prefix — e.g. 1042.
Commas in the search are ignored — you don't need to type them.

Quick-editing a contact field

On a member card, click any email or phone value (or the — dash if empty) to open a small edit popup. Update the value and click Save.

Editing a full member record

  1. Click the Edit button in the green header of a member card.
  2. A modal opens with all fields — first name, last name, member number, both emails, both phones.
  3. Make changes and click Save Changes.

Adding a new member

  1. Click + Add Member at the top right of the Member Search page.
  2. Fill in the first name, last name, member number, and any contact details.
  3. Click Save. The member is added to the directory and will appear in the autocomplete on checkout and restring forms.

Automatic phone number updates

When a member submits a phone number during a demo checkout or restring form, the system automatically updates their contact record in the directory:

  • If Phone 1 is empty → the number is saved as Phone 1.
  • If Phone 1 is taken and Phone 2 is empty → saved as Phone 2.
  • If both are already on file with different numbers → Phone 1 is overwritten with the new number.
  • If the number already matches either phone on file → nothing changes.

Equipment & QR Codes

Adding new equipment

  1. Click Equipment in the top nav, then Add New Equipment.
  2. Enter the name, category (racquet or paddle), and up to five spec fields (e.g. Head Size, Weight, String Pattern).
  3. Add any notes and click Save. A QR code is generated automatically.

Editing equipment

  1. On the Equipment list, click Edit next to the item.
  2. Update the details and click Save.

Retiring equipment

On the Equipment list, click Retire to remove an item from the active kiosk without deleting its checkout history. Retired items can be reactivated at any time.

Marking equipment under maintenance

On the Equipment list, click Under Maintenance to flag an item as temporarily unavailable. It will show as unavailable on the kiosk but remain in the active list.

QR codes

  • Each piece of equipment has a unique QR code that links directly to its checkout page.
  • To print a QR label, click Print QR on the equipment list or equipment edit page.
  • To print all QR codes at once, click Print All QR Codes on the Equipment page. Equipment notes are shown in red below each QR for reference.
  • If the server URL ever changes, use Regenerate All QR Codes to update them all at once.

History & Reports

Demo checkout history

Click History in the top nav to see a full log of all past demo checkouts, including who had each item, when it was checked out, and when it was returned.

  • Use the search bar to filter by customer name or member number.
  • Click Unmark Returned on any record to revert it back to an active checkout if it was marked returned by mistake.

Restring history

The bottom section of the Restringing page shows all picked-up jobs. Use the Picked Up search bar to filter by customer name or member number.

Reports

Click Reports in the top nav for usage summaries, including most-checked-out equipment and most active members.

Activity Log

Click Activity in the top nav to see a timestamped log of all staff actions — checkouts, returns, restring status changes, edits, and deletions. Each entry shows which staff member performed the action.

Use the activity log to trace any changes if something looks incorrect.
